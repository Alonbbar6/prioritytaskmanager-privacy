## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I create a task?
Tap the **+** button on any screen to add a new task. You can set a title, notes, priority level (A-E), and urgency/importance for the Covey Matrix.

### What is the ABCDE Priority Method?
- **A** — Must Do: Serious consequences if not completed
- **B** — Should Do: Mild consequences if not completed
- **C** — Nice to Do: No real consequences
- **D** — Delegate: Can be done by someone else
- **E** — Eliminate: Should not be done at all

### What is the Covey Matrix?
The Covey Matrix (also known as the Eisenhower Matrix) organizes tasks into four quadrants based on urgency and importance:
- **Q1:** Urgent & Important — Do first
- **Q2:** Not Urgent & Important — Schedule time for these
- **Q3:** Urgent & Not Important — Delegate if possible
- **Q4:** Not Urgent & Not Important — Consider eliminating

### How do I use Groups and Schedules?
Groups let you organize related tasks together with recurring schedules. Create a group, add time blocks for specific days, and your schedule will appear on the Schedule tab timeline.

### How do I use the Breathing Exercise?
Open any task's detail view and tap **Breathing Exercise**. Follow the guided inhale, hold, and exhale cycle to reset and refocus.

### Where is my data stored?
All your data is stored **locally on your device only**. Nothing is sent to any server. See our [Privacy Policy](https://alonbbar6.github.io/prioritytaskmanager-privacy/) for details.

### How do I delete my data?
- Delete individual tasks from the task detail screen
- Go to **Settings > Clear All Data** to erase everything
- Uninstalling the app removes all data permanently

### How do I enable notifications?
The app will ask for notification permission on first launch. If you declined, go to **Settings** in the app and tap **Enable** next to Notifications, which will take you to iOS Settings to grant permission.
