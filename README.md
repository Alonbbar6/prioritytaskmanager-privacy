# Privacy Policy for Priority Task Manager

**Last Updated: February 13, 2026**

## Introduction

Priority Task Manager ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our iOS application.

## Developer Information

**Developer:** Alonso Bardales
**Contact:** alonsobardales.apps@gmail.com
**App Name:** Priority Task Manager
**Bundle ID:** com.alonsobardales.PriorityTaskManager

## Information We Collect

### Task Data
The app stores the following information you provide:
- Task titles and descriptions
- Priority levels (A, B, C, D, E)
- Urgency and importance ratings
- Due dates and completion status
- Task notes
- Scheduled time blocks and group schedules

### Local Preferences
- App settings and preferences (e.g., onboarding completion status)

**Important:** All data is stored **locally on your device only**. We do NOT collect, transmit, or store any of your data on external servers.

### What We Do NOT Collect
- Your name, email address, or any personal contact information
- Location data
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Analytics or usage data
- Contacts, photos, or any other device data
- Any personally identifiable information

## How Your Data Is Stored

### Local Storage Only
- All task data is stored on your device using iOS local storage (UserDefaults)
- Your data **never leaves your device**
- There is no cloud storage, no server, and no remote database
- No data is transmitted over the internet

### iOS Security
- Your data is protected by the built-in security features of iOS
- Data is sandboxed within the app and inaccessible to other apps

## Data Sharing

**We do NOT share, sell, rent, or transmit your data to any third parties.** There are no third-party services, SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks in this app.

## Notifications

The app requests permission to send local notifications to remind you of scheduled tasks and group events. These notifications are generated entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

## Your Rights

### Access Your Data
- All your data is visible within the app at all times

### Delete Your Data
- Delete individual tasks within the app
- Use "Clear All Data" in Settings to erase everything
- Uninstalling the app removes all data permanently

### Modify Your Data
- Edit or update any task information at any time within the app

## Data Retention

- Your data exists only on your device for as long as the app is installed
- Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data
- There is no cloud backup or remote retention of your data

## Children's Privacy

Priority Task Manager does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since all data is stored locally and never transmitted, no personal information is collected from any user regardless of age.

## Third-Party Services

This app uses **no third-party services**:
- No analytics platforms
- No advertising networks
- No cloud databases
- No social media integrations
- No tracking tools

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
